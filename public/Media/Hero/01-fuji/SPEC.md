# Scene 01 — Mount Fuji (山)

**Meaning:** stability, legacy, perspective.
**The feeling:** "I am above uncertainty, looking toward a permanent foundation."

## Required footage — real aerial only

- Camera high above an **unbroken sea of clouds**, sunrise or early morning
- Mount Fuji **distant on the horizon** — roughly 12–18% of frame width,
  base fully hidden in cloud, rising above the cloud line
- **Horizon level, in the lower third**; sky owns two-thirds of the frame
- Telephoto compression; layered cloud strata with visible atmospheric haze
- Movement: slow lateral drift, gentle push, or locked hold — gimbal-stable
- 12–25 s usable; seamless loop preferred; 4K master if available

## Reject

Close-up summit shots · orbiting the peak · looking down at the mountain ·
airline-window frames/reflections · rotor blur · contrails · lens flare ·
tilted horizon · burnt-in timecode or watermark · tourist framing (mountain
centred and large)
