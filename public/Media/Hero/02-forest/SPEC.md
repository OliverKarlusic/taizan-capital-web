# Scene 02 — Japanese Forest (森)

**Meaning:** patience, discipline, cultivation.
**The feeling:** planted in rows by people who never sat in the shade.

## Required footage — real forest only

- Inside a Japanese **cedar (sugi) or bamboo** forest — tall straight
  trunks receding into mist, shafts of low light
- A **cultivated** forest reads better than wild growth: the order in the
  planting is the point of the scene
- Movement: slow dolly between trunks, slow vertical tilt, or locked hold
  with drifting mist — stabilised
- Light: early morning, soft, diffused, visible atmosphere
- 12–25 s usable; seamless loop preferred; 4K master if available

## Reject

People · paths with signage · shrines/temples (that's a different scene) ·
autumn tourist colour · handheld shake · fast pans · hard dappled sunlight
that will strobe under overlaid text

## Search terms

"sugi cedar forest mist Japan", "bamboo grove fog slow dolly",
杉林 霧 (Aflo / amanaimages), Arashiyama bamboo (crowd-free takes only)
