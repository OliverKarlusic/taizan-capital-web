# Scene 03 — River (川)

**Meaning:** compounding, continuous growth.
**The feeling:** water does not hurry, yet nothing withstands it.

## Required footage — real water only

- Clear moving water over stone — a mountain stream or calm river reach
- Mid or close framing where the flow is **continuous and unbroken**;
  steady laminar movement, not turbulence
- Movement: locked-off hold or a very slow push — the motion should come
  from the water, not the camera
- Light: overcast or early morning; soft, low-contrast
- 12–25 s usable; a seamless loop is **strongly** preferred here — water
  makes loop cuts more visible than cloud does
- 4K master if available

## Reject

Waterfalls · whitewater/rapids · people · bridges or built structures ·
concrete channels · long-exposure "silky water" looks · over-saturated
turquoise grading · harsh specular sparkle (reads as cheap stock)
