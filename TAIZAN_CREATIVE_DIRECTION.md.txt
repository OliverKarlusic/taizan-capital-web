# Taizan Capital Creative Direction

The website should feel like entering a cinematic Japanese environment.

Do NOT create:
- video game environments
- procedural mountains
- fake 3D terrain
- fantasy landscapes

The goal is:
- National Geographic aerial footage
- luxury documentary cinematography
- realistic drone footage

Mount Fuji is not an object.
Mount Fuji is an entire environment.

Camera:
- above the clouds
- looking towards the snowy summit
- distant helicopter/drone perspective
- slow and stable movement

Environment:
- massive sky
- realistic cloud formations
- atmospheric depth
- sunrise lighting
- snow-covered summit

The feeling:
- calm confidence
- discipline
- long-term thinking

Mount Fuji represents:
- permanence
- foundation

Clouds represent:
- uncertainty

Above the clouds represents:
- perspective
- clarity

Prioritise realism over complexity.

If true 3D is unavailable:
use:
- real footage
- photographic layers
- depth
- parallax
- cinematic transitions

Never create a fake mountain model.
